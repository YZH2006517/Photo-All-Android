ZRetouch offline adaptive color enhancement

The bundled classifier and three learned LUT basis tensors are exported without
retraining from Hui Zeng's Image-Adaptive-3DLUT sRGB pretrained checkpoints.

Source: https://github.com/HuiZeng/Image-Adaptive-3DLUT
Paper: Hui Zeng, Jianrui Cai, Lida Li, Zisheng Cao, Lei Zhang.
Learning Image-adaptive 3D Lookup Tables for High Performance Photo Enhancement
in Real-time. IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence.

The accompanying LICENSE.txt reproduces the upstream Apache License 2.0.
manifest.json records source revision, checkpoint and exported-asset hashes.

This mode runs a compact CNN and learned color transformation locally.
It does not run the original VeraRetouch or SmartPhotoCrafter large models.

Additional models in this app:

Zero-DCE++, Chongyi Li, Chunle Guo and Chen Change Loy
Source: https://github.com/Li-Chongyi/Zero-DCE_extension
Upstream use: academic research, CC BY-NC 4.0
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
Modification: the original Epoch99 curve predictor is exported to ONNX. The
published curve is applied to the full output image in the Android pipeline.

FFDNet, Kai Zhang, Wangmeng Zuo and Lei Zhang
Source: https://github.com/cszn/KAIR
License: see LICENSE-KAIR.txt.
Modification: original RGB weights are exported to an even-size ONNX graph;
the Android pipeline handles tile padding and combines the results.

SepLUT-S, Canqian Yang and coauthors
Source: https://github.com/ImCharlesY/SepLUT
License: Apache-2.0, reproduced in LICENSE-SepLUT.txt.
The official FiveK-sRGB small checkpoint is exported with all learned weights.
Android applies the generated 1D and 3D LUTs with the upstream interpolation.
seplut-model.json records the source revision and asset hashes.

NeurOp, Yili Wang and coauthors
Source: https://github.com/amberwangyili/neurop
License: MIT, reproduced in LICENSE-NeurOp.txt.
The official FiveK-Lite checkpoint is exported as a strength predictor and
three sequential pointwise operators for black clipping, exposure and vibrance.
Android interpolates a 65 x 65 x 65 RGB lattice evaluated by those operators.

AdaInt, Canqian Yang and coauthors
Source: https://github.com/ImCharlesY/AdaInt
License: Apache-2.0, reproduced in LICENSE-AdaInt.txt.
The official FiveK-sRGB checkpoint is exported as a predictor for the 3D LUT
and its nonuniform sampling vertices. Android applies the upstream adaptive
trilinear interpolation to all output pixels.

additional-color-models.json records the NeurOp and AdaInt checkpoint
provenance, exported asset hashes and numerical export audit.

Reference matching uses color statistics inspired by Reinhard et al.,
Color Transfer between Images (DOI: 10.1109/38.946629), in Oklab as defined
by Björn Ottosson (https://bottosson.github.io/posts/oklab/).
